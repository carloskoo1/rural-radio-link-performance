# Academic biographies of the six manuscript authors

These biographies were supplied by the corresponding author for the research documentation. Professional categories are reported as supplied; institutional verification may be required in the journal's author workflow. No photographs are included.

## Carlos J. Koo Labrín
**Email:** ckoo@unc.edu.pe

Carlos J. Koo Labrín received the B.S. degree in Electronic Engineering from Universidad Ricardo Palma, Peru, in 1996, and the M.S. degree in Telecommunications and Networking from Universidad Tecnológica del Perú in 2015. He is currently a Principal Professor at Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Peru, where he also serves as Coordinator of the Advanced Telecommunications Research Laboratory. His research interests include wireless communications, radio propagation in high-altitude environments, microwave-link design, telecommunications networking, and the application of machine learning to telecommunications systems.

## Néstor E. Muñoz Abanto
**Email:** nestor.munoz@unc.edu.pe

Néstor E. Muñoz Abanto received the B.S. degree in Systems Engineering from Universidad Alas Peruanas, Peru, in 2010. He is currently an Associate Professor at Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Peru, where he participates in research activities related to telecommunications and networking. His research interests include Internet of Things technologies, wireless communication systems, radio-link design in mountainous and high-altitude regions, and emerging technologies for rural connectivity.

## Víctor Sánchez Cáceres
**Email:** vsanchez@unc.edu.pe

Víctor Sánchez Cáceres received the B.S. degree in Statistics and the M.S. degree in Systems Engineering from Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería, Peru, in 1992 and 2004, respectively. He is currently a Principal Professor and collaborator with the Advanced Telecommunications Research Laboratory at Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Peru. His research interests include statistical modeling, predictive analytics, data analysis, machine learning, and applications to engineering and telecommunications systems.

## Marisol Tapia Romero
**Email:** mtapia@unc.edu.pe

Marisol Tapia Romero received the B.S. degree in Systems Engineering from Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca and the Ph.D. degree in Systems Engineering from Universidad Nacional de Piura, Peru. She is currently an Associate Professor at Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Peru. Her research interests include emerging telecommunications technologies, wireless systems, data-driven approaches, and innovative technologies applied to complex engineering and communication problems.

## José Camilo Micha Ortiz
**Email:** jmicha@unc.edu.pe

José Camilo Micha Ortiz received the Ph.D. degree in Systems Engineering from Universidad Nacional de Piura, Peru. He is currently a faculty member of the School of Systems Engineering at Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Peru. His research interests include information systems, business intelligence, data-driven decision support, systems engineering, and computational methods applied to organizational and engineering problems.

## Rosel Burga Cabrera
**Email:** rburga@unc.edu.pe

Rosel Burga Cabrera received the B.S. degree in Computer and Systems Engineering from Universidad San Pedro, Peru, and the Ph.D. degree in Systems Engineering from Universidad Nacional de Piura, Peru. He is currently a Principal Professor at Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Peru. His research interests include systems engineering and communications, information and communication technologies, machine learning, data mining, business intelligence, and computer networks.

**Affiliation shared by the six authors:** Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Cajamarca, Peru; Advanced Telecommunications Laboratory.
