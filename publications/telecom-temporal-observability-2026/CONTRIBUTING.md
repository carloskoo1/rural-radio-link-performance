# Scientific change control

The source-of-truth data are immutable. Do not add uncontrolled measurements or directly edit past published tables. Propose changes with a new branch/commit and clearly describe the original provenance, any transformation, the algorithm version, the SHA-256 of every changed artifact and the reason for each correction. Do not force-push or silently replace a public scientific revision.

Before accepting changes, verify read-only execution, unchanged private-data boundaries, source checksums, and reconciliation against Python/SQL outputs. Changes to authorship, funding, journal disclosures and contributors require confirmation with the corresponding author and the individuals concerned. No prospective field records are accepted into this completed retrospective article release.
