# Editorial clarification — 28 October 2026

The corresponding author clarified the following facts before submission of the manuscript *Temporal Observability of Wireless Backhaul: Evidence from Two High-Andean RF and TCP Field Campaigns* to *Telecom* (MDPI).

1. **University support** — Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca (UNC) provided antenna equipment as institutional in-kind support. The corresponding author provided and operated the radio-link infrastructure and financed the remaining experimental and infrastructure costs with personal funds. No external competitive research grant identifier was reported.
2. **Participation** — Luisita Adalid Mendo Urbina did not participate in this article and must not be described as an author, co-investigator, acknowledged contributor, or pending article authorship candidate. The six UNC faculty authors are the only authors listed for this manuscript; individual CRediT roles still require their documented confirmation.
3. **Independent data provenance** — the frozen 3×2 RF/TCP campaign remains one of the empirical datasets analyzed. Its acquisition dates, experimental schedule, hashes and audited measurements are unchanged. Attribution about other, separate academic work is not determined by this article's authorship disclosure.

**Scope of correction:** editorial statements only. No modification was made to source measurements, analyses, code, SQL, derived tables, figures or frozen dataset hashes. The initial `v1.0.0-rc1` tag, original `release_manifest.json` and initial `SHA256SUMS.txt` remain archival records of the earlier release; consult current `main` commits and `SHA256SUMS_EDITORIAL_CORRECTION.txt` for the later corrected editorial text.
