## Relationship to the journal manuscript

This deposit archives reproducibility software, derived results, the experimental schedule, audit evidence, metadata and figures associated with the manuscript titled *Temporal Observability of Wireless Backhaul: Evidence from Two High-Andean RF and TCP Field Campaigns*. The article is **not yet published in a journal** and does **not share** the Zenodo software DOI as an article DOI. In the paper, cite the record as supporting software / supplementary materials, not as a peer-reviewed publication.
