## Author metadata

### Carlos J. Koo Labrín
Email: ckoo@unc.edu.pe
ORCID: 0000-0002-0323-2961

Biography:
Carlos J. Koo Labrín received the B.S. degree in Electronic Engineering from Universidad Ricardo Palma, Peru, in 1996, and the M.S. degree in Telecommunications and Networking from Universidad Tecnológica del Perú in 2015. He is currently a Principal Professor with the Universidad Nacional de Cajamarca, Peru, where he also serves as Coordinator of the Advanced Telecommunications Research Laboratory. His research interests include wireless communications, radio propagation in high-altitude environments, microwave link design, telecommunications networking, and the application of machine learning techniques to telecommunications systems.
