# Cover Letter - IEEE Access

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Associate Editor,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript, “Beyond RSSI: Configuration-Dependent Performance and Temporal Stability in a Long-Distance Point-to-Point Wireless Link,” for consideration as a Research Article in IEEE Access.

The manuscript reports a field-based experimental study of a long-distance point-to-point wireless link operated under six frequency-bandwidth configurations. The analysis jointly evaluates received signal strength (RSSI), signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), modulation and coding scheme (MCS), observed throughput, temporal variability, and robustness to temporal aggregation. The study uses non-parametric group comparisons, effect-size estimation, a comparative stability index, and Multiscale Temporal Robustness Analysis (MTRA).

The manuscript's contribution is the integration of these complementary dimensions in a reproducible field-analysis workflow. In particular, the study distinguishes mean performance from temporal stability and evaluates whether configuration-associated differences remain detectable at 15-, 30-, and 60-minute aggregation scales. The manuscript explicitly limits interpretation to the evaluated link and observation periods and does not claim universal causal effects of frequency or bandwidth.

The supporting research artifacts are maintained in a version-controlled repository containing scenario organization, processing scripts, statistical outputs, evidence tables, publication figures, and reproducibility documentation:
https://github.com/carloskoo1/rural-radio-link-performance
